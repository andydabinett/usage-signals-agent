   and `query.wikidata.org/robots.txt` has `Disallow: /sparql`. Spec §3 says `/w/api.php` isn't disallowed; that's wrong for generic bots. The client skipped all
   20 `wbsearchentities` calls (logged as robots skips). Result: no employees, industry or country for any account.
   *Possible fix (needs your OK):* hand-map Wikidata QIDs in a seed, then fetch `/wiki/Special:EntityData/<QID>.json`, which robots explicitly allows
   (`Allow: /wiki/Special:EntityData/*.`). Or a human fills `seeds/manual_snippets.csv` with employee ranges.
2. **SEC EDGAR returned 403 "Your Request Originates from an Undeclared Automated Tool" on all 20 Form D searches**, even with
   `CONTACT_EMAIL` in the User-Agent (`usage-signals-agent/0.1 (+https://github.com/andydabinett/usage-signals-agent; <email>)`).
   Per D11 the client didn't retry with different headers. The cause is unconfirmed: either the User-Agent format (SEC's example is "Company Name email") or a block on
   the box's cloud IP. No funding rows.
3. **GDELT:** 10 of 20 queries got 429 even at 1 request per 5 s (after 3 backoff retries). The other 10 returned `{}` (no articles). No news rows.
   This is a rate limit, not a terms-of-service block; a slower re-pull (for example 1 per 10–15 s) would probably work.
